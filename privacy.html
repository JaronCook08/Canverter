Privacy Policy

Your data should not require a calculator to understand.

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Canverter may collect, use, and share information when you visit or interact with the website. Canverter does not require user accounts and does not ask you to submit financial account information.

Information you provide

When you type a purchase description or price into the calculator, that information is processed in your browser to generate a result. Canverter does not intentionally transmit or store those calculator entries on its own servers.

If you email Canverter, the email provider will process the information you voluntarily include, such as your email address, message, and attachments.

Information collected automatically

The website host and enabled service providers may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from an IP address, and timestamps. This information may be used for security, diagnostics, analytics, site operation, and performance improvement.

Analytics

Canverter may use Google Analytics or a similar analytics service to understand how visitors use the website. Analytics services may use cookies or comparable technologies and may collect usage and device information. Analytics will only operate when it has been enabled and when any legally required consent has been obtained.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Canverter may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

Google and its advertising partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visits to Canverter and other websites. You may manage personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings and other available industry opt-out tools.

Cookies and consent choices

Cookies are small files or browser storage entries used for functions such as security, preferences, analytics, and advertising. Where required, Canverter will present a consent choice before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website features or ad delivery.

How information may be shared

Information may be shared with hosting, security, analytics, advertising, and email service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a reorganization or transfer of the project.

Canverter does not sell calculator entries or maintain a database of the purchases you type into the calculator.

Data retention

Canverter retains information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to provider settings and legal requirements. Emails may be retained to respond to requests, maintain records, prevent abuse, and improve the site.

Children's privacy

Canverter is a general-audience entertainment website and is not directed to children under 13. Canverter does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact Canverter to request review or deletion.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or consent withdrawal. To submit a privacy request, email canverterfeedback@gmail.com. Canverter may need enough information to reasonably verify and process the request.

External links

Canverter may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website, its providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Privacy questions may be sent to canverterfeedback@gmail.com.
