About Canverter

A calculator built from boredom, caffeine, and questionable judgment.

Canverter is the most useful financial calculator you never knew you needed.

How it started

Canverter began one night when its creator was bored, wanted to practice web development, and hoped to make a few people laugh. Instead of measuring purchases in ordinary dollars, the idea was to translate them into units that feel much more important: Monster Energy drinks, Red Bulls, and Costco hot dogs.

What it is

Canverter is an entertainment website. It takes a price you provide, divides it by a representative local retail price, and gives the result a little personality. It is not a budgeting service, financial planner, price tracker, or substitute for professional advice.

Where it may go

Canverter may eventually become the starting point for more joke websites and playful web experiments. Possible additions include more converters, seasonal converters, a random purchase generator, share and copy tools, conversion history, more themes, and perhaps a mobile app.

Those ideas are possibilities, not promises. For now, the original three converters are doing important work.
