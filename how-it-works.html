How It Works

Highly advanced division, presented with unnecessary confidence.

Canverter turns a dollar amount into a ridiculous but understandable comparison.

Step 1: Enter a purchase

Type a price or a sentence containing a price, such as “Should I buy a $3,000 gaming PC?” Canverter looks for the purchase amount in your message.

Step 2: Choose a converter

Select Monster Energy, Red Bull, or Costco hot dogs. Each converter has a representative unit price.

Step 3: Divide and judge

Canverter divides the purchase price by the selected unit price.

purchase price ÷ converter unit price = estimated quantity

The result is rounded for readability and may be accompanied by a randomized introduction, variety, topping, or verdict.

Where the unit prices come from

Energy drink prices are static estimates based on average retail prices observed in the creator's area. The Costco hot dog converter uses the familiar combo price. Taxes, promotions, product sizes, retailers, locations, and time can all make real prices different.

Why prices are not live

Canverter is designed first as a quick entertainment tool, not a real-time shopping database. Static prices keep the experience fast and predictable. If feedback strongly favors live pricing, it may be considered later.

Possible future features

Ideas under consideration include more converters, special seasonal options, a random purchase generator, share and copy tools, conversion history, additional themes, and a mobile app.

Important: Canverter results are estimates for entertainment and should not be used as financial advice or as a substitute for checking real prices.
